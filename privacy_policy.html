STATVORO Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026 · App: STATVORO (Android & iOS)

Home Privacy Policy Terms of Use

STATVORO (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides free football analysis and informational insights, plus optional in-app donations. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and your choices. By using STATVORO, you agree to this policy.

18+ only. Informational, not a betting service.

STATVORO is for adults. Insights and statistics are for information and analysis only. Informational analysis only. No outcome is guaranteed. STATVORO does not place bets, does not operate a sportsbook, and does not take wagers.

1. Information we collect

Without an account (default use)

  • App preferences stored on your device (theme, odds format, 18+ confirmation, notification toggle).
  • Device notification token if you enable notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Public content — football insights and results read from our database. No profile is required.

If you create an optional account

  • Email address and authentication data processed by Firebase Authentication (Google).
  • Account records in Firebase, such as synced favorites and device registration for notifications.

In-app donations

  • Payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google Play, depending on your device.
  • We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • The stores may share limited purchase status with us so we can complete the transaction.
  • Donations are voluntary. They do not unlock extra insights, premium content, or extra features.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

2. How we use information

  • Show daily informational insights, history, and app features.
  • Send push notifications when you opt in.
  • Authenticate optional accounts and sync preferences you choose to save.
  • Process voluntary in-app donations through Apple or Google billing.
  • Protect the service (security rules, abuse prevention, optional App Check).
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-party services

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, and optionally App Check).

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Apple payments are subject to Apple’s privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Google Play billing: Google Payments privacy notice

4. Data retention

  • Local preferences remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Account data is kept while your account exists.
  • You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account. That removes your Firebase Authentication account and associated account records we control (favorites, devices, and related user documents).
  • Public insights and results may be retained as historical content and are not personal accounts.
  • Apple or Google may retain purchase records under their own policies.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off notifications in Settings.
  • Use the app without signing in.
  • Sign out in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings (in-app). This is the primary deletion path required by Apple.
  • If in-app deletion is unavailable, email mrclinci30@gmail.com.
  • EEA/UK users may have rights to access, correct, or erase personal data.

6. Security

We use encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), Firebase security rules, and secure on-device storage for sensitive identifiers. No method is completely secure.

7. Children

STATVORO is not directed at anyone under 18. The app asks you to confirm you are 18 or older before use. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. Contact us if you believe a minor has provided personal information.

8. International transfers

Firebase may process data in countries where Google operates data centres. Google describes its safeguards in its documentation.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
